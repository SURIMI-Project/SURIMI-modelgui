Verified on 2026-11-11. Local WebP derivatives, resized to 640px wide at quality 80.
Images are representatives of marine life, not assertions of regional exclusivity.
The Aegean uses the brief's European anchovy alternative rather than mackerel.

These are underwater photographs rather than transparent fish cutouts. CSS masks
blend their edges into the card's marine background; no white rectangular borders
are introduced. Photographers are credited in image tooltips. Attribution is not
required by the chosen releases, but retained here for provenance.

A separate Chicco Caroli anchovy photo was investigated but was not used because
the download was rate-limited. No image with uncertain licensing was substituted.
